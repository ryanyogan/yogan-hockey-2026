# Rylan appears as "Rylan Yogan" with invented stats and no real schedule

Rylan is a youth player. The site and this project call him Rylan Yogan, which is not his legal name, and his page shows only his club's name, invented over-the-top stats and invented game rows. Nothing is pulled from his league, and his real schedule and opponents are never shown, because a public site should not let a stranger match a child to a league listing or learn where he will be and when.

The fictional "Rylan Yogan" on the Maple Leafs roster from the Parity Reference is kept and presented as the same person: his family page links to it as his career projection.
